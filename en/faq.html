Choosing a system

How do I choose the right system for my home?

It depends on floor area, insulation, room layout, ceiling height and your winter heating needs. We do an assessment to recommend the best system: wall-mounted, multi-zone or central.

How many BTU do I need?

It depends on:

  • floor area and ceiling height;
  • insulation quality;
  • orientation (sunlight);
  • room layout.

Proper sizing is essential for comfort and efficiency.

What is the difference between a wall-mounted and a central system?

Wall-mounted (mini-split): ideal for specific zones or a home without ductwork.

Central: distributes air throughout the house through the ducts.

What are the best systems for Québec’s climate?

We recommend systems designed for good cold-climate performance, high efficiency and a solid warranty. The choice depends on your budget and available subsidies. We work with several brands: GREE, Zephyr, Canair, Elios, RunTru, Arcoair, Directair, Ameristar and Lennox.

Does an air conditioning and heating system work well in winter?

Yes. Modern systems, properly selected and installed, work efficiently even through Québec winters.

Can you replace an old system or air conditioner?

Yes. Our technicians remove the old equipment professionally, then install the new system, cleanly and to code.

Can I install a system in a condo or duplex?

Yes, depending on building regulations and available space. We analyze your project to propose a compliant and efficient solution.

Maintenance and repairs

Do you repair air conditioning and heating systems?

Yes: diagnostics, repairs, emergency service and performance optimization.

How often should maintenance be done?

At least once a year. Maintenance extends the system’s life and improves its efficiency.

Why is my system not heating or cooling well?

Possible causes:

  • dirty filters;
  • wrong settings;
  • lack of maintenance;
  • wrong sizing;
  • a technical problem.
Why is my system leaking or noisy?

It can come from drainage, the installation or a component. An inspection is recommended.

Do you offer fast service?

Yes, depending on availability and urgency.

How can I extend my system’s life?

Regular maintenance, proper use, filter cleaning and professional service.

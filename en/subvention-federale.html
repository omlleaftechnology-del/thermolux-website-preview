National Heat Pump Rebate

The Government of Canada is investing $2 billion to help up to 820,000 households install a cold-climate heat pump. No energy audit required.

Eligibility

Who can benefit?

Here are the main points. The program is not final yet: criteria may change.

  • Homeowner in Canada, not necessarily of the primary residence (a rented property qualifies).
  • Eligible heat pump: identified by its AHRI number on the official product list.
  • Purchased and installed by an HVAC contractor.
  • One rebate per household, even if you own several properties, and no earlier federal funding for the address.
  • Current heating: electricity (baseboards, furnace), natural gas, oil, propane, diesel, coal or wood.
  • Emergency or planned installations are both accepted; no proof of breakdown needed.

Send your invoices

Funding is only guaranteed once the application is approved. If you install during the retroactive period, ask your installer to confirm the system is sized to heat the whole home. For homes heated with natural gas, a heat pump may not lower the bill: it depends on the region and the system.

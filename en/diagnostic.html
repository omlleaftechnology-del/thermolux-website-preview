FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should I get a diagnostic?

If the system no longer heats or cools, makes noise, starts and stops often, has weak airflow, or if your electricity bills go up.

How is it different from a repair?

The diagnostic identifies the cause of the problem. The repair fixes it, using the solution we choose with you.

What should I do in an urgent breakdown?

Call 438-509-1823. Emergency repair is available.

Do you diagnose central systems?

Yes, wall-mounted and central systems in houses with ducts.

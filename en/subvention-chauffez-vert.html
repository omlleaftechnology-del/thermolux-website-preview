Chauffez vert: program ended

The Government of Québec aid for replacing a central oil or propane heating system has not been offered since March 31, 2026.

Contact usOfficial website
Program status

Ended March 31, 2026

Work had to be finished and the application filed before that date.

This page keeps the amounts for reference. To replace oil heating today, see the federal conversion program (applications closed, final receipts by January 31, 2027), LogisVert and the 2027 national rebate.
Light oil

Amounts granted

Converting a central heating system fuelled by light oil.

  • Detached house$1,275
  • Mobile home$1,075
  • Semi-detached or row house$875
  • Duplex or triplex$875 × eligible units
  • Multi-unit residential building$550 × eligible units
Propane

Amounts granted

Converting a central heating system fuelled by propane.

  • Detached house$850
  • Semi-detached or row house$650
  • Mobile home$600
  • Duplex or triplex$650 × eligible units
  • Multi-unit residential building$225 × eligible units
Water heater

Converting a water heater

Eligible if done at the same time as the heating conversion.

  • Light-oil water heater$250
  • Propane water heater$200
  • Duplex, triplex or building× eligible units or × water heaters installed

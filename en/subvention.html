The program

Help for homeowners

With LogisVert, you get financial support when you install an energy-efficient heating or cooling system, such as a high-performance system.

Eligibility and the amount of help are always verified with Hydro-Québec. Rules change: confirm them before you start work.

Eligibility

Who can benefit?

Here are the main points of the residential program. Hydro-Québec has the final say.

  • Owner of an eligible home in Québec, served by Hydro-Québec or by an independent, municipal or cooperative network.
  • Home lived in year-round: single-family house, condo, multiplex, building of 19 units or fewer, or an apartment in a larger complex.
  • New equipment: new indoor and outdoor units, listed among the program’s approved systems.
  • Licensed installer: a company registered in the Québec enterprise register and holding the appropriate RBQ licence.
  • No exclusions: for example, an outdoor unit in a non-habitable area or a partial replacement of an existing system is not eligible.

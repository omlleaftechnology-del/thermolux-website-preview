The service

Two types of installation, the same care

The wall-mounted system suits houses, condos and apartments. It heats and cools, with excellent energy performance and low noise.

The central system goes in homes that have ventilation ducts and spreads an even temperature throughout the house.

Either way, we install high-efficiency units built for the Québec climate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Wall-mounted or central system?

Wall-mounted suits houses, condos and apartments. Central is ideal for a house with ventilation ducts. We will advise you during the quote.

Which cities do you install in?

Montréal, Saint-Laurent, Laval, Longueuil, Brossard, Terrebonne, Blainville, Repentigny, Saint-Eustache, and across the North Shore and South Shore.

Rénoclimat: ventilation rebate

Since October 1, 2026, Rénoclimat no longer funds heating. The rebate for a heat-recovery air exchanger remains: $500.

Air exchanger

The conditions to meet

See our air exchangers →

  • Type of unit: heat recovery, listed in section 3 of the Home Ventilating Institute certified products directory.
  • Installation or replacement: both qualify.
  • Other work required: the aid is paid only if the home’s EnerGuide rating improves between two consecutive energy evaluations.
  • An exchanger alone can increase energy use and make the home ineligible: combine it with other energy-efficiency work.
New rules

What changed

The program no longer accepts appointment requests for a pre-work energy evaluation under the old terms. If your home already had an evaluation, finish the work and get the post-work evaluation before March 31, 2027 to keep the old terms.

For a new heating system, see LogisVert or the National Heat Pump Rebate instead.

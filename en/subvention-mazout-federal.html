Eligibility

Who was eligible?

  • Canadian homeowner heating the home with oil.
  • Household income at or below the median after-tax household income.
  • Integrated electricity grid: remote areas (including some in Québec) and Nunavut are not eligible.
  • Paid upfront in most cases, before you pay for the work.
What it covers

Up to $10,000 for

The purchase and installation of an eligible heat pump, and if required:

  • necessary electrical and mechanical upgrades;
  • safe removal of the oil tank (removal is not required by the program, but is an eligible cost);
  • a supplemental electric heating system;
  • switching other oil-using systems, such as a water heater;
  • outdoor piping loops for ground-source heat pumps.
January 31, 2027: deadline to upload your final receipts and the attestation form signed by your contractor in the Canada Greener Homes portal. All work must be completed and paid for. Questions: enviroinfo@ec.gc.ca or 1-800-668-6767.
